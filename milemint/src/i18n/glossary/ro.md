# Romanian (ro) glossary

Audience: Romanian-speaking drivers and couriers working in the UK (and other English-speaking countries). They use English at work, so English tax-form names stay as they are.

## Form of address

Informal **tu** throughout (imperatives like *Glisează*, *Atinge*, *Sortează*; *contul tău*, *cursele tale*). Modern Romanian consumer apps (Revolut, Glovo, Bolt, Spotify) talk to users with *tu*. It is warmer and shorter, and it suits the light, joking tone of the reminders. Polite requests after errors use *Te rugăm să…* (“please”), which is still *tu*.

## Terms

| business (trip type) | **de lucru** | Everyday and fits self-employed couriers. *De serviciu* sounds like an employee on an assignment, and *business* is an anglicism. Toggle: *De lucru* / *Personală*. |

| business mileage / business miles | **mile de lucru** / **km de lucru** | Matches “de lucru” above. |
| business purpose | **scopul cursei** (help text: *scop de lucru*) | Short and clear in field labels. |
| sort (mark business/personal) | **a sorta** (*Sortează*, *nesortată*) | Used in everyday Romanian, short, and works for “classify” too. |
| swipe | **a glisa** (*Glisează la dreapta/stânga*) | Apple’s Romanian term for swipe. |
| tap | **a atinge** (*Atinge*) | Apple’s Romanian term. |

| money back | **bani înapoi** | Everyday. |

Plurals use the CLDR categories one / few / other. *other* (20 and up, e.g. 20, 21, 100) adds **de**: *1 cursă, 5 curse, 20 de curse*; *1 milă, 3 mile, 100 de mile*. The abbreviation “km” takes no “de”. The small countdown-box unit (`days`) uses *zile* for both few and other, because “de zile” under a number looks odd.

iOS strings used (best knowledge of Apple’s Romanian iOS):
- Allow While Using App: *Permite când se folosește aplicația*
- Change to Always Allow: *Schimbă la Permite întotdeauna*
- While Using the App: *Când se folosește aplicația*
- Always: *Întotdeauna*
- Never: *Niciodată*
- Ask Next Time Or When I Share: *Întreabă data viitoare sau când partajez*
- Location: *Localizare*
- Open Settings: *Deschide Configurări*
- ALLOW LOCATION ACCESS: *PERMITE ACCESUL LA LOCALIZARE*
- Settings → Notifications: *Configurări → Notificări*

Jokes were adapted. Examples: “Knock knock” became *Cioc, cioc* / *Cine-i acolo?*, which is also a Romanian joke format. “Sunday scaries” became *Stres de duminică seara?*. The dating pun stays as *Glisează la dreapta pentru economii* (swiping right is understood in Romania). “Free money alert” became *Alertă: bani gratis*. “bank the deduction” became *pune deducerea la pușculiță* (piggy bank). “every penny” became *până la ultimul bănuț*.

## Unsure

- All the iOS strings listed above, apart from *Întotdeauna*, *Niciodată*, *Localizare* and *Configurări*. I am fairly sure of those four. The others most need checking against a real iPhone set to Romanian, especially **“Allow While Using App”**, **“Change to Always Allow”**, **“While Using the App”** and the **“ALLOW LOCATION ACCESS”** header. Keep the “Location is set to “While Using”…” line in step with them.
- “UK” in the half-width country tile is *Regatul Unit* (12 characters). If it doesn’t fit, *UK* or *Marea Britanie* are alternatives.
- “Business errand” as *Drum de serviciu*: *Comision* is colloquial but also means a fee.
- “Neither” as *Niciuna* and “Set hours” as *Program fix* (work-pattern choices).
- “{{count}} selected” uses *{{count}} selectate* for the other form too. Grammar would want “de” at 20+, but *20 de selectate* sounds unnatural in a UI counter.
- “{{label}}: earned” as *{{label}}: obținută*. The feminine agrees with *realizare* (milestone). Change it if milestone titles are read as masculine nouns.
- “found this tax year” under a money figure is *suma găsită în acest an fiscal*, chosen to avoid gender agreement with £/$.
- “Shifts & rounds (delivery apps)” as *Ture și livrări (aplicații de livrări)*: “rounds” has no exact courier term.
- Shift cheers (*Să înceapă jocul!*, *Hai că pornim!*, *Pornim la drum!*): these are adaptations, not literal translations.
- Round 3: *Portchei iCloud* as Apple’s Romanian name for iCloud Keychain, and *Configurări → numele tău → iCloud* as the path. Check on a Romanian iPhone.
- Round 3: *Ridicată* for the “Higher” tax band (HMRC guidance in Romanian sometimes uses *cota superioară*).
- Round 3b: “Enter amounts as numbers, e.g. 2400 or 2,400.50.” is now *Introdu sumele ca numere, de ex. 2400 sau 2400,50.* The logbook parser accepts a decimal comma, so the *cu punct la zecimale* instruction was dropped.
- Round 3: “Client visit · area” is quoted in English (*„Client visit · zona”*) because the stored label isn’t translated. If the app starts translating stored labels, change it to *Vizită la client · zona*.
- Round 3: “End early” as *Încheie acum* (the alert’s question already says *mai devreme*).
