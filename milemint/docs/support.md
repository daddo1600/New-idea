# MileMint Support

Need help with MileMint? Email **milemint.support@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you within two business days.

## Common questions

**MileMint isn't detecting my drives.**
Open **Settings → MileMint → Location** and choose **Always**, and make sure **Background App Refresh** is on. Low Power Mode can delay detection. You can always add a missed trip by hand with the **+** button.

**Will it drain my battery?**
MileMint uses low-power geofencing while you're parked and switches GPS on only while you're driving.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone, encrypted. We never receive your trips or location. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**How do I move to a new iPhone?**
Your trips are included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup. Restore that backup on the new phone.

**Is this tax advice?**
No. MileMint estimates deductions using the IRS standard mileage rate. Check with a tax professional for your situation.
