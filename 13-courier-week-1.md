# Courier push: week 1 (Mon 5 – Sun 11 Oct 2026)

**Aim of the week:** recruit the first **75 delivery couriers** into the "MileMint Riders" beta, turn **10** of them into ambassadors, and learn what couriers need, while v1.0 goes through App Review.

The app isn't on the App Store yet, so couriers join through a **public TestFlight link** (up to 10,000 testers). The only extra step for them is installing Apple's TestFlight app. At launch they move to the App Store version.

**Build to use:** 29 or later (vehicle type, shift mode, missed miles check).

## Targets for the week

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Conversations (in person + messages) | 150 |
| TestFlight installs | 75 |
| Couriers who start at least one shift | 30 |
| "MileMint Riders" ambassadors | 10 |
| Group admins agreeing to a pinned post | 5 |
| Couriers giving feedback (5-minute chat or voice note) | 20 |

## The offer to couriers

> **Founding Riders get MileMint Pro free for 12 months** when it launches (worth £59.99), plus a say in what we build next.

Delivered at launch as one-time Apple offer codes, one per Founding Rider on the tracking sheet. TestFlight users don't pay anything anyway.

## Monday: set up (about 3 hours)

- [ ] **Submit v1.0 for App Review** (build 29+, both subscriptions attached).
- [ ] **Public TestFlight link:** an external group "MileMint Riders" with the public link on, and build 29 submitted for Beta App Review (usually under 24 hours). Claude can set this up.
- [ ] **QR cards:** order 250 business-card size, next-day local print or online (~£25–40). Artwork below; the QR points to the public TestFlight link.
- [ ] **Tracking sheet** (Google Sheets), columns: Date · Name / handle · Where met (spot or group) · Platform (Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Just Eat, Amazon Flex, Evri, other) · Vehicle · Area · Installed? · Started a shift? · Feedback · Ambassador? · Referred by · Language
- [ ] **Find 20 groups.** Search Facebook for "[your town] delivery drivers", "Deliveroo riders UK", "Uber Eats drivers UK", "Amazon Flex UK", "Evri couriers", "Just Eat couriers [town]". Ask the first couriers you meet which WhatsApp groups they're in. Note each group's rules on promotion.
- [ ] **Map your hotspots** (20 minutes on Google Maps). Look for:
  - town-centre and retail-park clusters of McDonald's, KFC, Nando's, Greggs, Wagamama and big supermarkets, where riders wait for orders;
  - your nearest Amazon delivery station, where Flex drivers queue 20–30 minutes before block start times;
  - Evri and DPD depots.

  Pick **3 spots** within 20 minutes of home.

## Tuesday: online (about 2 hours)

- [ ] **Message 10 group admins** (template 3). Offer a pinned post plus 5 free years of Pro to give away to their members.
- [ ] **Post in groups that allow it** (template 2); most allow a post if it's genuinely useful and not spam.
- [ ] **Reddit:** one helpful post each in r/deliveroos, r/UberEATS and r/AmazonFlexDrivers (template 5). Answer questions for a day; don't just drop a link.
- [ ] **Personal WhatsApps** to anyone you know who delivers or knows someone who does (template 1).

## Wednesday: in person, lunch peak (11:30–14:00) at spot 1

- [ ] Approach couriers **who are waiting**, never mid-delivery or riding. Stay on the public pavement, keep doorways clear, and be friendly and brief (script below).
- [ ] Hand a card to everyone; help install on the spot if they're keen (TestFlight, then the app, about 90 seconds).
- [ ] Ask each one: "What's the most annoying thing about tracking your miles?" and note it on the sheet.
- [ ] **Evening (17:30–20:30) at spot 2**, the dinner peak and the busiest time.

## Thursday: ambassadors and follow-up

- [ ] Message everyone who installed: "Did your first shift log OK?" Fix or note anything broken.
- [ ] **Recruit 10 MileMint Riders ambassadors** from the keenest (template 4):
  - they get Pro free for a year at launch;
  - they get first say on features;
  - later, a free month per friend who subscribes.

  Ask each to share in their own WhatsApp groups and note on the sheet who they bring.
- [ ] **Amazon Flex station:** 20–30 minutes before a morning block start. Flex drivers sit in their cars waiting, so it's a good moment for a card through the window, with a smile.

## Friday: content

- [ ] With 1–2 ambassadors, film three 20–30 second clips on a phone, with no faces needed. Examples:
  1. "What Deliveroo counted vs what MileMint logged": the **missed miles check** screen after a few shifts. This is the hero clip.
  2. "Start shift, deliver, end shift: every mile logged." A screen recording.
  3. "How much can a courier claim? 55p a mile…" Talk to camera or text on screen, always "estimated".
- [ ] Post to the groups that agreed, and to a MileMint TikTok or Instagram account (make one if needed).

## Saturday: in person, busiest dinner rush (17:00–21:00) at spot 3

- [ ] Same approach as Wednesday. Weekend nights are the busiest for food delivery, with lots of waiting riders.
- [ ] Bring 50+ cards. If a group of couriers is together, give a quick demo of the missed miles screen on your phone.

## Sunday: review (45 minutes)

- [ ] Update the sheet and fill in the targets table.
- [ ] Read all feedback and list the **top 3 problems** for Claude to fix next week.
- [ ] Which channel worked best: in person, groups, admins, Reddit or ambassadors? Put next week's time there.
- [ ] Thank every ambassador personally.

## Scripts and templates

### In person (20 seconds)
> "Hiya, sorry to catch you while you're waiting. Do you claim your mileage on your tax return? … Your app only counts miles with an order on board. The drive to the restaurant and home are claimable too, and most riders miss them. I've built an app that logs all of it automatically. It's free for riders who join now, and founding riders get Pro free for a year. Here's a card: scan it when you've got a sec."

If they're interested, show the missed miles screen. If not: "No worries, have a good shift!"

### Template 1: personal WhatsApp

It logs every mile automatically (even between orders, which Uber Eats and Deliveroo don't count) and works out what it's worth at tax time. Founding testers get Pro free for a year. Would you give it a go, or do you know anyone who delivers? [TestFlight link]

> I've built **MileMint**, an iPhone app that logs every mile automatically. Tap **Start shift** and everything until you finish counts. It's private: your trips stay on your phone and nothing is sold.
> It's in beta. **Founding Riders get Pro free for 12 months at launch.** Join here: [link]
> Happy to answer any questions below. (Admins: thanks for letting me post!)

## FAQs couriers will ask

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is it free? | Free plan: 40 automatic drives a month, and a whole shift counts as one. Pro: unlimited. Founding Riders get Pro free for a year at launch. |
| Does it drain my battery? | GPS only runs while you're moving. Parked, it uses almost nothing. |
| Do you sell my location? | No. Trips are stored encrypted on your phone. There's no account and nothing is sold. |
| I'm on a scooter or bike. | Set your vehicle to motorbike (24p a mile) or bicycle (20p a mile) in the UK. |
| I drive an Amazon van for a delivery company. | If you're employed and it's their van, the company covers the costs and you can't claim mileage. Self-employed Flex drivers in their own car can. |
| Android? | Not yet. iPhone first; Android is planned. Take their number for launch. |
| Is it tax advice? | No. Figures are estimates at HMRC rates. Check with an accountant if unsure. |

## Safety and conduct

- Only approach riders who are **waiting**. Never approach someone riding, loading or mid-delivery.
- Stay on public land. Keep restaurant doors and drive-thru lanes clear, and leave if staff ask.
- Wear something with the MileMint logo or carry the cards visibly so people know what you're about.
- No promises about tax savings; always "estimated".
