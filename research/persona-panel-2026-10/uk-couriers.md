# UK couriers: segment summary (n=70)

**Headline:** 34 of 70 (49%) use Android and can't install it. Mean NPS 5.0; NPS −74 overall, −50 on iPhone.

## Funnel
- **Download (iPhone, n=36):** 22 yes, 9 maybe, 5 no.
- **Using after 2 weeks:** 16 yes, 9 maybe, 6 no (23% of all).
- **Pay:** 16 (23%): 13 yearly, 3 monthly. Free only: 9. Won't pay: 11.
- **Price:** median ceiling £4/month, about the yearly plan. Only 3 accept £5.99 monthly. 17 Android users would pay once Android exists.

## What sells it
- The missed miles check (pickup runs, gaps, Flex station drives) shown in pounds.
- Shift mode: one shift counts as one drive, so the free plan works.
- No account. Cheaper than MileIQ and Driversnote. Portuguese, Romanian, Polish and Bengali.

## What kills it
- iPhone only shuts out half the segment.
- Bikes get 20p a mile and mopeds 24p; several riders expected 55p.
- Van owner-drivers claim actual costs or capital allowances, so mileage doesn't apply.
- Low earners often don't file Self Assessment.

## Top 5 feature requests
1. Android app (15)
2. Delivery-platform integration: Flex blocks, Uber import, tag by platform (5)
3. Earnings and tips tracking, like Gridwise (4)
4. Backup and sharing with an accountant (4)
5. Urdu, Somali or Arabic, and Albanian (3)

## Top 5 objections
1. Android only (34)
2. Van owners already claim actual costs (8)
3. Battery drain on 10–12 hour shifts (6)
4. Bike and moped rates too low (6)
5. "Always" location for an unreviewed app (5)

## Quotes
- "It showed me £40 of miles Uber never counted in a fortnight." (Uber Eats driver, 37)
- "Once you claim capital allowances on the van you can't switch to mileage." (DPD owner-driver, 48)
- "Gridwise is free and does earnings too, so why would I pay six pound for just miles?" (Flex, 35)
- "After fuel I make maybe nine pound an hour, so six pound a month feels like a lot." (Just Eat, 38)
- "It's 24p for the moto, not 55p, but still, it adds up." (Deliveroo, 26)
- "My wife has iPhone, I have Android, so she would get it and I wouldn't." (Flex, 46)
