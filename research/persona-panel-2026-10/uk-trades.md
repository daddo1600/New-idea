# UK self-employed & small-business drivers (n=70)

**Headline:** strong need, weak fit. 43% are on Android, and many vans are company-owned or on capital allowances, so 55p doesn't apply.

- **Download:** 23 yes, 12 maybe, 5 no, 30 can't (Android). iPhone: 58% yes.
- **Kept 2 weeks:** 17 yes, 10 maybe (49% of triers; 24% of panel).
- **Pay:** 18/70 (26%): 15 yearly, 3 monthly. Avg max £4.30/mo. NPS -76 (iPhone only: -57).

**What sells it:** money people never claimed (cleaners, hairdressers, carers, own-car trades). Ltd directors get a clean claim to their company. High-mileage reps get the 55p/25p split done for them, and Uber drivers get the missed-miles check. Data stays on the phone.

**What kills it:** it's iPhone only. Company vans and vans on capital allowances are locked out of the flat rate. Instructors and cabbies claim actual costs. MTD users already in QuickBooks/Xero don't want a silo. Window cleaners and dog walkers make lots of short hops that flood the log.

**Price:** £49.99/yr reads as "less than one call-out" for heavy drivers. £5.99/mo is resisted. 40 free drives last a tradesperson 5–8 days, so for them it's a paid app.

**Top feature requests:** 1) Android 2) Xero/QuickBooks/FreeAgent export for MTD 3) actual-costs / van mode 4) client/job tagging 5) Ltd-director reimbursement report.

**Top objections:** 1) no Android 2) van can't use 55p 3) "my accountant does it / wants Xero" 4) free tier too small 5) "Always" location, battery, unknown solo founder.

**Quotes**
- "Half the lads on site are on Samsungs, so come back when it's on Android."
- "Company van, company fuel card, so there's literally nothing for this app to claim."
- "I've never claimed a single mile and it found me £600 in my first month, which made me feel a bit sick."
- "If it doesn't go into Xero my accountant won't touch it."
- "Ten pickups a day means I'd blow through 40 free drives by Thursday, so it's basically a paid app for me."
- "Uber said 18,000 miles, it found 26,000, so that's about two grand more in expenses."
