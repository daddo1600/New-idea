# Australia: 55 personas

**Numbers:** 36 iPhone and 19 Android, so 35% can't install it. Download: 14 yes, 16 maybe, 6 no. Still using after 2 weeks: 13 yes, 10 maybe. 11 would pay (20%): 9 yearly, 2 monthly. Most they'd pay: median about A$6.50 a month. NPS is −78 (mean 4.4; no promoters).

**What sells it:** hands-off logging with work-hours rules. The missed-km check showed real Uber Eats and Flex undercounting. The language options (PT, ES, HI, ZH) and having no account both help. Low-km sole traders (hairdresser, tutor, groomer, junior agent) who had never claimed were converted by seeing a dollar total.

**What kills it:** the 5,000 km cap. Full-time rideshare drivers, tradies, rural nurses and reps go past it within weeks. They need the **12-week logbook method**, which Driversnote and Logbook Me already have. Rideshare drivers lodge **GST/BAS**, and the app ignores fuel GST. Utes over 1 t, vans, novated leases and company cars are ineligible. Most migrant riders are on Android.

**Price:** A$79.99 a year is acceptable to low-km professionals. A$9.99 a month looks like Driversnote money for less function. Couriers find the free tier enough.

**Top feature requests:**
1. 12-week logbook with business-use %
2. Android
3. BAS/GST quarterly report with fuel receipts
4. NDIS per-participant trip tags
5. Driversnote import and a PDF that tax agents accept

**Top objections:**
1. The cap undersells high-km drivers
2. iPhone only
3. No GST/BAS support
4. Ineligible vehicle (ute, van, lease, motorbike, e-bike)
5. "My accountant does it" and solo-founder risk

**Quotes:**
- "I do 45,000 k's a year mate, the 5,000 km cap is pocket change." (Uber, Parramatta)
- "Driversnote already does the ATO logbook, why would I switch?" (Uber, Gold Coast)
- "An app that only knows kms and not my fuel GST is a toy." (Uber, Perth)
- "Tag by participant and you've got me." (NDIS worker, Geelong)
- "A shift counts as one drive, so the free plan covers my whole month." (Eats/Flex, Brisbane)
- "Seeing $1,400 come up made it worth it straight away." (Hairdresser, Ballarat)
