# Canada segment (55 personas)

**Mix:** 31 iPhone / 24 Android. About half are gig drivers, including 11 Punjabi-speaking and 8 francophone personas.

## Headline numbers
- **Download:** 17 yes (31%), 11 maybe, 3 no, **24 can't (Android, 44%)**. Among iPhone users: 55% yes.
- **Still using after 2 weeks:** 11 yes (20% of panel; 35% of iPhone users).
- **Would pay:** 20 (5 monthly, 15 yearly). Only 4 accept C$7.99/month; most cap at C$5–6, which fits the C$69.99/year plan.
- **NPS:** −82 overall, −68 among iPhone users. The highest score was 8.

## What sells it
The missed-km check, shift mode for Flex blocks, half MileIQ's price with no account, and the Punjabi UI.

## What kills it
iPhone-only, when Brampton/Surrey drivers are mostly on Android. **The per-km "found" total is wrong for most Canadians:** self-employed people and T2200 employees claim actual costs × business-use %, so accountants dismiss it. Logs live only on the phone, when CRA wants 6 years of records. There is no Revenu Québec or Québec French.

## Price reaction
C$69.99/year is acceptable. Monthly C$7.99 feels steep; Gridwise users anchor on free.

## Top 5 feature requests
1. Android app
2. Business-use % plus fuel/expense tracking (T2125/T777)
3. Backup and export
4. Revenu Québec TP-1 support in Québec French
5. CRA logbook sample-period mode, plus GST/HST guidance

## Top 5 objections
1. Can't install it on Android
2. The dollar figure isn't my deduction
3. Phone-only storage versus 6-year record retention
4. Ignores Québec
5. Unproven battery claim

## Quotes
- "Almost every driver I know in Brampton has a Samsung."
- "The 73 cents number is nice but it doesn't go on my return."
- "This showed me thousands of extra kilometres I was leaving on the table."
- "If I drop my phone off a ladder I lose the whole year's log."
- "If it's France French and never mentions Revenu Québec, it feels like an app made for somebody else."
- "CRA lets you do a base year then a three-month sample; if the app knew that it would sell itself."
