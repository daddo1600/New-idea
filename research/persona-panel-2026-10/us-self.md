# US self-employed & field workers (n=60)

**Phones:** 37 iPhone, 23 Android (38% can't install). **Download:** 14 yes, 15 maybe, 8 no (29/60 = 48%; 78% of iPhone users). **Still using after 2 weeks:** 10 yes, 13 maybe, 6 no. **Would pay:** 16/60 (27%; 43% of iPhone users), 13 yearly and 3 monthly; payers' average max is $5.50/mo. **NPS: -75** (avg 4.3).

**What sells it:** solo 1099 workers with many short drives (home-health RNs/PTs, adjusters, photographers, new realtors), plus people leaving MileIQ or Everlance. Auto-logging, the "$X found" total and no account land well.

**What kills it:** (1) iPhone only. (2) No team or employee tracking. Every owner with 1–5 staff said no. (3) No QuickBooks sync or accountant access. (4) W-2 reps and aides are reimbursed via Motus, Concur or CarData and can't deduct federally. (5) Phone-only storage. (6) Unknown solo developer with no reviews.

**Price:** $49.99/yr seems cheap next to MileIQ ($13.99) and Everlance ($8.99). The bigger issue is the 40-drive free cap: people making 6–8 short hops a day use it up in two weeks.

**Top 5 feature requests:** Android app; team/employee plan with mileage approval; QuickBooks (QBO/Solopreneur) sync and accountant sharing; encrypted iCloud backup; client, property or job tags.

**Top 5 objections:** iPhone only; no team or fleet support; already covered by QuickBooks, an employer system or fleet GPS; data lives only on one phone; brand-new app from an unknown developer.

**Quotes:**
- "MileIQ missed half my winter trips; if this catches them at a third of the price, I'm in." (consultant, MN)
- "QuickBooks already tracks my miles and puts them where my bookkeeper looks, so why add another app?" (contractor, TN)
- "I reimburse four cleaners for miles; I need to see their drives, not just mine." (cleaning owner, NC)
- "I'm W-2 and can't write off miles, so for me this is a pretty odometer." (travel nurse, CO)
- "During storm season I drive 4,000 miles a month; unlimited for fifty a year is a no-brainer." (adjuster, LA)
- "I'm not letting an app follow me around all day." (insurance agent, IA)
