# MileMint: brief for the simulated user panel

## What it is

An iPhone app (Android later) that logs drives automatically in the background and works out what each business drive is worth at tax time, using the official rate:
- 🇬🇧 HMRC: 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles, then 25p;
- 🇺🇸 IRS: 76¢ a mile;
- 🇨🇦 CRA: 73¢ a km for the first 5,000 km, then 67¢;
- 🇦🇺 ATO: 91c a km, capped at 5,000 km per car.

**No account, no sign-up.** Trips are stored encrypted on the phone and never leave it.

## Key features

- **Automatic logging:** each trip appears a few minutes after you park, with the route, the distance and its tax value. Location must be set to "Always"; set-up coaches users through the two iOS prompts.
- **Sorting trips:** swipe right for business, left for personal. Drives can start as business by default. Rules sort drives automatically: work hours, learned regular routes, commute detection.
- **Shift mode for couriers** (Uber Eats, Deliveroo, Amazon Flex, Evri, DPD and others): swipe to start a shift, and every drive until it ends is business. A whole shift counts as one drive on the free plan. Shows a cheer in local slang when you start ("Tally ho!", "Let's roll!").
- **Missed miles check for couriers:** enter the miles your delivery app counted, and MileMint shows the extra business miles it logged (the drive to the pickup, between orders, home again) and what they're worth. Shareable.
- **Home screen:** the tax-year total ("£2,217 found in the 2026/27 tax year"). Opens with a short animation counting up your real total, seasonally themed: Santa's sleigh in December, kangaroos in an Australian summer and so on.
- **Milestones and celebrations:** £50, £100, £250 found; 1,000 miles; first shift. Confetti, plus a share card with an App Store link.
- **Reminders and tax dates:**
  - a Sunday reminder to sort the week's drives, on by default;
  - a tax-year-end countdown with reminders 2 months, 1 month and 1 week out;
  - a return-deadline countdown;
  - a "Tax dates" guide per country, covering yearly returns and quarterly obligations (MTD, 1040-ES, CRA instalments, BAS).
- **Reports:**
  - a CSV export, free;
  - a PDF mileage report in the tax office's own wording, Pro;
  - odometer readings for CRA.
- **Vehicles:** a garage for multiple vehicles with registration plates, and correct rates for car or van, motorbike and bicycle (UK has motorbike and bicycle rates).
- **Manual trips:** add a missed trip with address search.
- **Language:** English, Spanish, Portuguese (BR), French, Romanian, Polish, Hindi, Punjabi, Bengali and Simplified Chinese.

## Pricing

- **Free:** 40 automatic drives a month (a whole shift counts as one), unlimited manual trips, CSV export.
- **Pro:** unlimited automatic drives and the PDF reports.
  - US: $5.99 a month, or $49.99 a year with a 30-day free trial.
  - Other countries: roughly £5.99 / £49.99, C$7.99 / C$69.99, A$9.99 / A$79.99.
- **Referral:** give a friend a free month; get a free month when they start paying (coming).

## Honest unknowns

- **Battery use:** the app is designed to be light and only uses GPS while driving, but there's no long real-world data yet.
- **Track record:** it's a brand-new app with no reviews and a small solo founder.
- **Platform:** iPhone only for now.
