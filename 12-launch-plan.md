# Launch plan: getting MileMint its first users

**Goal by 31 January 2027** (the UK Self Assessment deadline): **1,000 installs, 300 weekly active drivers, 60 paying Pro users, 50+ ratings averaging 4.7★.** Small numbers, but they prove the app works, give the App Store listing credibility, and are the evidence to take to cafés, accountants and Teams prospects.

## Principles

1. **Start where you have an unfair advantage: the UK, your area and your network.** You can meet people face to face, you know the local groups, and you're the founder. That beats any ad budget early on.
2. **One audience first: tradespeople and self-employed drivers in the UK** (electricians, plumbers, builders, cleaners, carers, driving instructors, mobile hairdressers, estate agents). They drive for work every day, claim 55p a mile, and many still use a paper log or nothing.
3. **Personal before scalable.** The first 100 users come from conversations, not ads. Ads and creators come once people are staying and rating.
4. **Ride the calendar.** Self Assessment is due **31 Jan** (UK), and the US tax season runs **Jan–Apr** (see `09-social-campaigns.md`). Canada's is Apr–Jun and Australia's is Jul–Oct, so plan those for 2027.

## Phase 0: before launch (now → App Store approval, ~2–3 weeks)

| Task | Why |
|---|---|
| **Submit version 1.0 for review** (latest build, both subscriptions attached) | Nothing below works until the app is live |
| **Domain + one-page site**: what it does, screenshots, "Download on the App Store", privacy and support pages, Teams waitlist | Somewhere to send people; looks credible |
| **"Founding 50" TestFlight group**: friends, family, local tradespeople you know. Ask for bugs and one sentence of feedback each | Real-world testing, first testimonials, first ratings on day one |
| **30-second screen recordings** (×3): the launch animation, a trip being swiped business, the report export | Reused everywhere: WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, the site |
| **Referral phase 1**: one Apple offer code, "a friend's first month free" | Every early user can bring someone |
| **In-app: ask for a rating at a happy moment** (first £100 found, after a report export) | Ratings drive App Store ranking and trust |
| **In-app: a shareable "brag card"**: "I've found £412 in mileage this month with MileMint", branded, one tap to WhatsApp or Instagram Story | Free, organic reach that looks good |

## Phase 1: launch month ("Founding 100")

| Channel | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your own network** | Personal WhatsApp messages, not a broadcast: "I've built an app for people who drive for work. Would you try it and tell me honestly what you think?" Then a founder post on LinkedIn and Facebook with the story and a 30-second video |
| **Local Facebook groups** | Tring, Hemel, Berkhamsted, Dunstable, St Albans and Aylesbury community and trades groups. Post as a local founder asking for testers, not an ad. Read each group's rules; many allow a "local business" day |
| **Trade communities** | UK trades Facebook groups, electricians' and plumbers' forums, r/smallbusinessuk, r/ContractorUK, r/UKPersonalFinance (help first; mention the app only where allowed) |
| **In person** | Trade counters (Screwfix, Toolstation, plumbers' merchants), cafés near industrial estates: a small counter card with a QR code. Ask the manager first |
| **Accountants and bookkeepers** | The biggest multiplier. Every sole-trader client needs a mileage log. Offer local bookkeepers free Pro for themselves and a code for their clients. One accountant can bring 20–50 users |

**Target:** 100 active drivers and 25 ratings by the end of the month.

## Phase 2: Self Assessment season (Dec → 31 Jan)

| Channel | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Content: "What are your miles worth?"** | Short videos and posts: "55p a mile: how much a plumber doing 12,000 business miles can claim", "What HMRC actually wants in a mileage log", "Missed miles: how much people leave unclaimed". Always "estimated"; never promise tax savings |
| **Micro-creators (UK trades TikTok, Instagram)** | 5–10 creators with 5K–50K followers: sparkies, plumbers, cleaners, driving instructors. Small fee per video plus a bonus per paying user through their offer code |
| **Accountant partners** | Their January rush is our moment: "Send clients MileMint for next year's log" |
| **Apple Search Ads (UK)** | £5–10 a day on "mileage tracker", "mileage log", "HMRC mileage" and competitor names, **only Dec–Jan** |
| **Referral push** | "Give a mate a free month": in-app banner and WhatsApp share |
| **US (from January)** | Start the US plan in `09-social-campaigns.md` at small scale; use UK results to decide how hard to push |

## After January

- **Perks pilot** (`11-growth-ideas.md`) in your area, now that there are local users to show cafés.
- **Referral phase 2 and Teams** once the domain, server and early revenue are in place.
- **Plan Canada** for its spring tax season and **Australia** for its July–October tax time.

## Weekly rhythm (30 minutes every Monday)

| Measure | Where | Healthy early target |
|---|---|---|
| Installs | App Store Connect → Analytics | Growing week on week |
| Still logging drives in week 2 | Sunday check-in / in-app counts (privacy-friendly, opt-in) | 40%+ |
| Trials → paying | App Store Connect → Subscriptions | 25%+ of trials |
| Ratings and reviews | App Store Connect | 4.7★+, reply to every review |
| Cost per paying user | Spend ÷ new paying users | Less than 3 months of Pro (≈£18) |

**Keep or kill:** after two weeks, stop any channel that brings no paying users, and double down on the best one.

## Budget (bootstrapped)

| Item | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Domain, site, email | ~£5 |
| Counter cards and leaflets (one-off) | ~£30 |
| Apple Search Ads (Dec–Jan only) | £150–300 |
| Creator fees (from Dec) | £100–300 |
| **Total** | **£0–600 a month**, mostly in the Dec–Jan peak |

## First six weeks, week by week

| Week | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Submit for review. Register the domain; site and privacy page live. Invite the Founding 50 on TestFlight. |
| 2 | Record the three videos. Collect tester feedback and fix the top issues. Create the friend offer code. |
| 3 | **Launch.** Personal WhatsApp messages (~100), founder post, first two local Facebook groups. |
| 4 | Trade forums and Reddit. Call five local bookkeepers. Counter cards at two trade counters. |
| 5 | First content posts. Reply to every review. Ask happy users for a brag-card share. |
| 6 | Check the numbers. Choose the best channel; line up creators for December. |
