# MileMint Social Media Campaign Ideas (US)

## What the evidence says works

- **Cal AI** (≈$30M+ ARR, acquired by MyFitnessPal) grew mainly through **high-volume creator partnerships** rather than big ad budgets: 150+ creators on retainer, each posting about 4 times a month. Winners were scaled into Spark Ads.
- **Show the result in under 3 seconds.** Cal AI's rule: if a creator can show the result instantly on screen, cost per install drops. **Our 3-second moment is the big "Deductions found: $X" number.**
- **Gig-driver content is already a huge genre on TikTok and YouTube:** daily earnings videos, "can you still make money in 2026", app comparisons. We slot into formats that already get views instead of inventing new ones.

## Seven campaign ideas

### 1. "What my miles were really worth" (main campaign)
- **Format:** gig creators already post "I made $142 today on DoorDash". We add one beat at the end: open MileMint and show **"+$38.76 in deductions today."**
- **Hook lines:** "Everyone shows what they earned. Nobody shows what they saved." / "The $38 DoorDash didn't pay me."
- **Why it works:** it fits their existing video with no script to change, and the payoff is a number on screen.

### 2. "Tax season panic" (Jan–Apr)
- Skits: *"POV: it's April 14 and you didn't track a single mile."* Then the reveal: *"20,000 miles × 72.5¢ = $14,500 you can't claim without a log."*
- Paired with a **free web calculator**: "How much did untracked miles cost you?" It captures emails and helps Google search too.

### 3. "Start 2027 right" (late Dec to 1 Jan launch)
- New Year's framing: *"Your 2027 mileage log starts on Jan 1. The IRS won't accept one you rebuild in April."*
- **Launch offer:** 30-day trial, plus a free extra month for each friend referred (referral link in-app).

### 4. Head-to-head tests (trust builders)
- **"Same drive, two phones"**: MileMint vs a competitor, filmed honestly. Did both catch it?
- **"My battery after a 10-hour shift"**: a time-lapse.
- ⚠️ Comparative claims must be real, repeatable test results. We publish the method.

### 5. "IRS myth busters" with a tax pro
- Partner with a CPA or Enrolled Agent creator for 30-second answers:
  - "Is your commute deductible?" (no)
  - "Do you need a log or just total miles?" (a log)
  - "Can you rebuild it later?" (not reliably)
- This builds authority and matches our commute-warning and audit-proof features.

### 6. Beyond gig drivers: "Day in the life" creators
- Plumbers, cleaners, home-care workers, realtors and mobile groomers post day-in-the-life content. End with the day's deduction total.
- These users have the **highest willingness to pay** and less competition for their attention.

### 7. Community, not ads
- **Facebook groups** (very active for gig drivers and realtors) and Reddit (r/doordash_drivers, r/uberdrivers, r/couriersofreddit, r/tax).
- Stay useful: answer mileage-tax questions and share the free calculator. Follow each community's self-promotion rules; no spam.
- **Quarterly tax-date posts** (Apr 15, Jun 15, Sep 15, Jan 15) keep us visible outside tax season.

## Creator programme (the engine)

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Who | 30–50 micro-creators (5K–100K followers): gig drivers first, then trades, realtors, tax pros |
| Pay | Small flat fee per video plus a bonus per paid subscriber, tracked by a unique link or code |
| Their audience gets | An extended free trial through their code |
| Sourcing | A fresh TikTok account that only engages with gig/trades content, so its For You page becomes a creator feed (the Cal AI method) |
| Scaling | Track installs → trials → paid per creator. Double down on the top 20% and turn their best videos into Spark Ads. |
| Owned channels | 1–2 faceless TikTok/Shorts/Reels accounts posting daily: deduction math, myth busters, screen recordings |

## Timeline

| When | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Nov–mid Dec** | Waitlist page and calculator live. Seed 10 creators with TestFlight builds. Collect testimonials. |
| **Late Dec–1 Jan** | App Store launch. "Start 2027 right" push. |
| **Jan–Apr 15** | Peak: tax-season panic, myth busters, creator programme at full volume, Apple Search Ads on competitor names. |
| **May–Dec** | Lower spend. Quarterly-tax reminders, trades/realtor creators, referral loop. |

## Rules we follow

- **FTC:** creators must disclose paid posts (#ad / "paid partnership").
- **No tax advice promises.** Say "deductions found / estimated", never "you'll save $X". Add "consult a tax professional".
- **Comparative claims** only from documented tests. We never make claims about competitors' data practices unless they're verifiable.

## What to measure

Cost per install, install → trial %, trial → paid %, and paid subscribers per creator. All attributed through RevenueCat, using creator codes and links.
