All sources accessed 2026-10-02. IMPORTANT METHOD NOTE: WebFetch to primary brand sites (found.com, upside.com, keepertax.com, everquote.com) was BLOCKED by the network egress proxy in this session, so most payout figures below come from search-result snippets, often from AGGREGATOR directories (getlasso.co, linkclicky.com, uppromote.com, affpaying.com, FlexOffers listings), not the brands' own terms. ## 9. US compliance basics

### Takeaway
(a) FTC: affiliate links are a material connection that must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously (2023 Endorsement Guides). (b) Insurance: in most PLMA states a licensed producer may pay unlicensed referrers only if the referrer does not discuss policy terms and the fee is not contingent on a sale; several states also cap the amount (often "de minimis", ~<$50; Tennessee $25; New York has no dollar cap). (c) Banking: the CFPB treats fintech marketing service providers as "covered persons" for UDAAP and sponsor banks are held responsible for partner marketing.

### Cited Findings
- FTC updated the Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) on 29 June 2023 — first major revision since 2009; published in the Federal Register 26 July 2023. — [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2023/07/26/2023-14795/guides-concerning-the-use-of-endorsements-and-testimonials-in-advertising); [Morgan Lewis](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2023/07/ftc-updates-endorsement-guides-proposes-endorsement-related-rule)
- "Clear and conspicuous" means difficult to miss and easily understandable by ordinary consumers in the target audience; affiliate links are a material connection; guides also cover procuring/suppressing/boosting consumer reviews. — [Perkins Coie](https://perkinscoie.com/insights/update/ftc-finalizes-updated-guidance-influencer-and-consumer-review-programs); [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2023/07/federal-trade-commission-announces-changes-to-the-endorsement-guidelines); [FTC business guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/node/28270)
- FTC proposed (June 2023) a Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (note: finalised in 2024 per general knowledge — not verified from source this session). — [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2023/07/federal-trade-commission-announces-changes-to-the-endorsement-guidelines)
- Insurance referral fees to unlicensed persons are generally legal but restricted in amount, conduct and tie to sale; under PLMA states a producer may pay an unlicensed referrer if not violating anti-rebating laws and the referrer does not solicit/negotiate/sell. — [Insurance Journal, Feb 2024](https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2024/02/19/761025.htm)
- Common conditions: referral does not include discussion of specific policy terms; compensation not based on purchase of insurance; many states require fee be de minimis (generally <$50). New York has no dollar limit; Tennessee caps at $25. — [Insurance Journal 2024](https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2024/02/19/761025.htm); [NY DFS OGC 06-01-14 (2006 — flag, older)](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/insurance/ogco2006/rg060114.htm); [NC DOI Referral Fee FAQ (2015 — flag, older)](https://www.ncdoi.gov/documents/agent-services/referral-fees-faqs/open); [Ohio Rev. Code 3905.18](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3905.18)
- Interagency Guidance on Third-Party Relationships: Risk Management issued June 2023; banks own risk of partners' consumer communications; CFPB's 2022 interpretive rule treats non-bank service providers whose marketing reaches consumers as covered persons under the CFPA (UDAAP). — [Luthor.ai 2026 (secondary)](https://www.luthor.ai/resources/partner-marketing-compliance-fintechs-bank-partnerships); [PerformLine](https://performline.com/blog-post/partner-banks-marketing-compliance-trends-you-need-to-know/); [OCC UDAP/UDAAP handbook](https://www.occ.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/unfair-deceptive-act/pub-ch-udap-udaap.pdf)
- Neobanks are not banks; "Member FDIC" attribution is treated by regulators as the sponsor bank vouching for surrounding claims (secondary source). — [Luthor.ai](https://www.luthor.ai/resources/partner-marketing-compliance-fintechs-bank-partnerships)

### Inferences
- MileSprout should: put an in-app affiliate disclosure adjacent to every partner offer; avoid saying a neobank "is a bank"; never discuss insurance policy terms/pricing; prefer per-click/per-lead (not per-policy) insurance payouts; and avoid passing a cash reward to users for insurance purchases (anti-rebating risk).
- Any "reward-to-user" sharing of bounties likely conflicts with many programmes' incentivised-traffic rules; must be cleared per programme.
