# UK Vehicle-Category Rewards / Affiliate Partners for MileSprout

Research date (access date for all sources below): 2 Oct 2026.

**Method and reliability caveat.** The network egress proxy blocked direct page fetches for almost every primary source tried: rac.co.uk, handbook.fca.org.uk, ui.awin.com, nextbase.co.uk, marshmallow.com and parkpm.co.uk (a PERG 5 PDF mirror). As a result, most findings below come from **web-search result snippets** of primary pages (Awin merchant profiles, brand affiliate pages, FCA Handbook) or from affiliate-directory aggregators (affilitizer, flexoffers, affscanner, paidonresults, linkclicky). Snippets are easy to take out of context. Snippets and directories often do not show the date a payout was published, so **every payout should be re-checked on the live programme page before outreach**. Where a figure comes only from an aggregator, the source says so. No contact emails or named managers could be verified from a primary page, so the contact column says "apply via programme page" throughout. No emails were guessed.

## Q1. Which UK vehicle partners pay best per referral and accept mobile app publishers?

### Takeaway
On published or snippet-visible rates, the strongest per-referral payouts are in **breakdown cover** (RAC about £20 roadside, AA £5 to £20 by policy value), **EV/energy** (Octopus "High Referrer" affiliate at £50 per customer) and **dashcams** (Nextbase 8% on about £170 AOV, roughly £13.60 per sale). Comparison sites pay little per click-out sale: MoneySuperMarket's standard rate is shown as £1 (one snippet says £4.20 for car), Confused.com about £1.84. None of the snippets confirmed explicit permission for in-app (mobile app) placement. App-publisher acceptance and incentivised-traffic rules must be confirmed in each Awin profile's "terms" tab after joining.

### Inferences
- For MileSprout's user base, ranked by likely value per referral: Octopus High Referrer (£50) is ahead of RAC/AA breakdown (£5–£20), then Nextbase (about £13.60 per sale), then BookMyGarage, Kwik Fit and Zuto (£3–£10), then comparison sites (about £1–£2 per converted click-out).
- RAC's carve-out for "non-cashback and loyalty" affiliates, together with MoneySuperMarket's bespoke cashback and loyalty rates, implies that passing the reward to the user is generally permitted but **priced differently**. Expect a lower CPA if MileSprout shares the commission with users as "rewards".
- Customer refer-a-friend schemes (Zego, Electroverse, Splend, Zoomo) are **not publisher programmes**. Their terms typically bar commercial or mass promotion. MileSprout would need a bespoke B2B partnership instead.

## Q3. Who are the reachable partnership contacts? And UK insurance-distribution rules

### Takeaway
No partnership email, press contact or named affiliate manager could be verified from a primary page in this pass, because direct fetches were blocked. For every partner: **apply via programme page**, listed below. On regulation: an app that only displays insurance information or links on an ongoing commercial arrangement is likely carrying on **insurance distribution**. An app that publishes a ranking or comparison list from which users can conclude a contract is in scope. An unauthorised business normally needs to become an **appointed representative**, and the **introducer appointed representative (IAR)** variant is the lightest option. Pure affiliate links via networks need legal checking against PERG 5.6.

### Cited Findings — FCA / insurance distribution
- The FCA defines insurance distribution activities to include "the provision of information concerning one or more insurance contracts... through a website or other media and the compilation of an insurance product ranking list, including price and product comparison, or a discount on the price of an insurance contract, when the customer is able to directly or indirectly conclude an insurance contract using a website or other media." — [FCA Handbook PERG 5](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/perg5) (search snippet)
- "If an introducer is an unauthorised person, they will need authorisation or exemption as an appointed representative." — [FCA Handbook PERG 5](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/perg5) (search snippet)
- Businesses that act only as introducers of insurance business are directed to PERG 5.6.2G–5.6.9G to decide whether they need authorisation. — [PERG 5.6](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/perg5/perg5s6) (snippet)
- Search summaries of PERG 5.6 also contained two statements that could not be checked against the Handbook text:
  - The exclusion does not apply to introductions that are part of an ongoing, pre-existing arrangement.
  - An introducer who merely suggests that a person seek advice from an authorised person, with no pre-existing agreement, is not making regulated arrangements.
  - Both are **unverified paraphrases**; check them against the Handbook. — [PERG 5.6](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/perg5/perg5s6)
- An introducer appointed representative is "an appointed representative whose scope of appointment is limited to effecting introductions to the firm... and distributing non-real time financial promotions which relate to products or services available from or through the firm". — [FCA Glossary: introducer appointed representative](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G585)
- An IAR cannot give advice, conclude contracts for the customer or do any regulated activity beyond introduction. — [Apex Insurance Brokers wiki: IAR](https://apexinsurancebrokers.co.uk/wiki/introducer-appointed-representative/) (secondary)
- Appointed representative rules are in SUP 12 and PERG 5.13. — [SUP 12](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/sup12); [PERG 5.13](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/perg5/perg5s13)
- Commentary notes the insurance AR regime is under change. — [O'Connors: Change afoot for the insurance AR regime](https://www.oconnors.law/news-views/change-afoot-for-the-insurance-appointed-representative-regime/) (date not confirmed)
- Comparison-site affiliate model: MoneySuperMarket pays only on click-outs that lead to a confirmed sale (CPA). The comparison site, which is FCA-authorised, carries out the distribution. — [Affilitizer: MSM](https://www.affilitizer.com/programs/moneysupermarket.com)

### Inferences
- **Lowest-risk route for MileSprout:** promote insurance only through **FCA-authorised comparison sites' or insurers' affiliate programmes**, using generic, non-ranked links ("Compare van insurance at X"). Avoid MileSprout's own price rankings, product recommendations or quote journeys. If MileSprout wants embedded quotes or a "recommended insurer" for couriers, it should seek an **IAR appointment** with a principal broker or insurer (e.g. a partner like Zego or a broker), or take legal advice.
- Paying users a reward for buying insurance may be treated as an inducement or financial promotion. Programmes such as RAC and MSM set separate cashback tiers, and any promotional copy about insurance in the app is a financial promotion that must be approved by an authorised firm.
- Breakdown cover sold as insurance (RAC, AA policies) falls under the same rules. Non-insurance categories (dashcams, MOT bookings, fuel cards, EV tariffs) do not raise insurance-distribution issues. Car finance (Zuto) is consumer credit broking, a separate FCA regime, and also needs review.

### Gaps
- The full text of PERG 5.6 could not be fetched to confirm exact paragraph numbers and the "mere provision of information / hyperlink" guidance. A solicitor or compliance consultant should confirm.
- No comparison-site affiliate T&Cs were read directly. Whether they permit app placement, push notifications or user rewards is unconfirmed.
- No press-office emails, phone numbers or named affiliate managers were verified for any partner.
