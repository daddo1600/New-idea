**Likelihood-of-confusion analysis**
- "MILE" is descriptive for mileage software, so examiners and courts will treat **SPROUT as the dominant element** of MILESPROUT.
- That makes any registered SPROUT mark covering software or financial/tax services in classes 9, 36 or 42 a potential citation. The risk is highest against a plain "SPROUT" registration for downloadable finance or expense software.
- Mitigating factor: crowding. With dozens of coexisting SPROUT marks in software and finance, each one has a narrow scope of protection, and small differences can be enough (the US "crowded field" doctrine; the UK/EU equivalent is weakened distinctiveness).
- Net assessment:
  - Outright refusal is possible but not likely in the US.
  - In AU, the risk depends on the Sprout Financial position.
  - An opposition from Sprout Social is unlikely given the different services. They are known to be active, though, so expect a watch notice.

### (c) MILE-formative marks in the mileage space
- MileIQ (Microsoft-owned, then spun out), Everlance, Driversnote, TripLog, MileLog (milelog.app), Mileage Log+, Mileage Track, Miles (travel rewards), Miles Go, Mile 1st and others.
- MILE- and MILES- prefixes are common, and the word is descriptive. No MILE-formative competitor shares the SPROUT element.
- The search did surface MILES-formative marks such as MILES PARTNERSHIP and MILES TECHNOLOGIES, but they are in unrelated fields.
- **Low risk**, except that the descriptive "MILE" part gives MILESPROUT less to rely on against SPROUT marks (see (b)).

## 3. Verdict: **CAUTION**
1. No official register could be reached. This is the main reason the result is not CLEAR. After losing the last name to a prior user, we should not rely on web indexing.
2. No exact or near MILESPROUT, MILE SPROUT or SPROUTMILES use or filing was found anywhere on the indexed web. That is a good sign.
3. SPROUT is crowded in software and finance (classes 9, 36 and 42). Because MILE is descriptive, SPROUT carries the mark. The most relevant risks:
   - a plain SPROUT registration for finance or budgeting software;
   - Sprout Financial in AU (Class 36);
   - Budget Sprout and other SPROUT finance apps;
   - Sprout Social's Class 42 portfolio (low probability, but an active brand owner).
4. Weak distinctiveness cuts both ways. MILESPROUT may coexist with other SPROUT marks, but it will also be hard to stop the next "XSPROUT".

It would become **BLOCKED** if the manual searches turn up:
- a live MILESPROUT, MILE SPROUT or SPROUTMILES filing in any target market; or
- a plain SPROUT registration covering mileage, expense or tax software or services in that market.

## 4. Manual searches (about 5 minutes each)
Search each of these strings: `milesprout`, `mile sprout`, `*sprout*` (wildcard), `sproutmile*`.

- **USPTO** (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov):
  1. Basic search for `milesprout`, then `"mile sprout"`.
  2. Expert mode: `CM:*sprout* AND IC:(009 OR 036 OR 042 OR 035) AND LD:true` (live records only).
  3. Read the goods and services of every hit that mentions software, expense, mileage, tax, accounting, budget or finance.
- **UK IPO** (https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext):
  1. Search by "Trade mark text", type `milesprout`, then `sprout` with "contains" matching.
  2. Filter to classes 9, 35, 36 and 42, status "Registered" or "Pending".
  3. Also tick "Include EU marks" for pre-Brexit comparable rights.
- **TMview** (https://www.tmdn.org/tmview):
  1. Search `*sprout*`.
  2. Filter Nice class 9/35/36/42, offices EM, GB, AU, CA, US and WO, status Filed/Registered.
  3. This one search covers most of the target offices.
- **WIPO Global Brand Database** (https://branddb.wipo.int):
  1. Brand name `sprout`, fuzzy matching.
  2. Filter Nice class 9, 36 and 42, status Active.
  3. Check for Madrid designations by Sprout Solutions (PH) and Sprout Social.
- **CIPO** (https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch): Search `sprout` in "Trademark", status "Active". Filter Nice 9/36/42.
- **IP Australia** (https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/search/quick):
  1. Quick search `sprout`.
  2. Advanced: Word contains `sprout`, classes 9, 36 and 42, status Registered/Pending.
  3. Specifically look for Sprout Financial.
- **Also check:**
  - App Store and Google Play for "Milesprout" and "Sprout mileage".
  - Companies House (UK) for "MILESPROUT".
  - Whether milesprout.com, .co.uk and .app are available. Do not buy anything yet.

## 5. Filing recommendation (only once the manual searches are clean)
- **File first in the UK, in classes 9 and 42.** It is cheapest, examination takes about 2 months, and the filing date then gives a 6-month Paris Convention priority for the US, CA and AU (and the EU).
  - Use a narrow specification, for example: "downloadable mobile application software for recording vehicle mileage and calculating mileage expenses for tax purposes" (Class 9), and "providing temporary use of non-downloadable software for mileage tracking and expense reporting" (Class 42).
  - Add Class 36 ("provision of financial information relating to tax deductions for vehicle mileage") only if the budget allows. It is the class most crowded with SPROUT finance marks, so it raises the opposition risk.
- **Then file in the US within 6 months,** claiming UK priority, in the same classes. File on an intent-to-use basis if the app has not launched.
- **Option:** a Madrid Protocol international application based on the UK filing, designating US, CA, AU (and EU later). This makes sense once you file in 3 or more countries.
