# Milesprout brand brief

*2 Oct 2026. Status: **working name, pending paid trademark clearance** (see `rename-shortlist.md`). Nothing in this brief should be published, bought or filed until clearance comes back, except the deliberate exceptions in §6.4.*

Inputs: `BRAND.md`, `14-launch-campaign.md`, `launch-playbook.md`, `uk-competitors-pricing.md`, `ui-panel-milemint-mileclear-mileiq.md`, `rename-shortlist.md`, the current icon (`milemint/assets/images/icon.png`) and the launch animation (`milemint/src/components/launch-intro.tsx`, `intro-scenery.tsx`).

**What stays the same:** the green gradient, gold, mint, cream, Inter, the plain voice, the road with lane dashes, the gold dot as the car, and the petrol/shops/café signs. **What changes:** the name, the mark (leaf → sprout) and the story the launch animation tells.

## 1. The idea

### One line
**The miles you drive grow into money you can claim.**

In the product and in ads, say **"money you can claim"**, not "money you keep". The counter shows a deduction, not cash in the bank. The UI panel's accountant (Helen) already flagged that "tax saved" overstates it, and BRAND.md says never claim what we can't prove. "Money you keep" can be the internal brand story. The public line says "claim".

### Why "sprout" beats "mint"
| | Mint | Sprout |
|---|---|---|
| What it says | Fresh, or minted money. Static: a thing you have. | Growth. Something that starts small and gets bigger every day you look at it. |
| Matches the product | Loosely. | Exactly: the home total grows with every shift. The launch animation literally grows. |
| Emotional job | "Clean finance app" (a crowded, slightly cold space). | "Something you're tending that pays you back." Gives a reason to open the app daily. |
| Our role | Money printer (overclaims). | Gardener: you drive, we make sure nothing you've earned gets lost. Fits "You'll know if a mile was missed". |
| For non-native speakers | "Mint" = herb, money, or "mint condition". Mixed signals. | "Sprout" is less common but the picture explains it instantly: a seedling is universal. The icon carries the name. |

"Mint" was also never ours (another company sells MileMint). Sprout lets the mark do more work than the leaf did: a leaf is finished, a sprout is on its way.

### Weaknesses, honestly, and how we handle each

| Weakness | Why it matters | How we handle it |
|---|---|---|
| **"Sprout" feels small or slow** | Couriers care about hundreds of pounds, not a seedling. "Slow growth" is the opposite of "see your money now". | **Always pair the sprout with a big real number.** The mark never appears alone on a hero; it sits next to the counter ("£412 found this tax year"). The animation is quick (1.6s climb) and the sprout grows *fast*: sprouting is the fastest growth stage of a plant, and we show it that way. In copy, "grow" is a verb about *their* money, never about us being early or small. Never use "little", "tiny", "baby" or "seedling" in copy. |
| **Brussels sprouts (UK)** | The UK's most-mocked vegetable. Christmas jokes are guaranteed, especially in a December/January campaign. | **Don't fight it, and never start it.** No sprout vegetables anywhere: the mark is a two-leaf seedling, never round or ball-like (that's another reason the gold dot sits *on* the stem, not as a round sprout head). Allow **one** knowing joke a year, on UK social only, in festive week: "The only sprout nobody leaves on the plate." Never in the app, the store listing or ads. Reply to jokes kindly and move on to the number. |
| **"Miles Prout" mishearing** | Said aloud, "Milesprout" can split as *Miles Prout* (sounds like a person's name). In **French, *prout* is the word for a fart**, and French is one of our 10 languages and the second language of Canada. That's the biggest risk in the name. | (1) **Write it one way only: "Milesprout"**, one word, capital M only. Not "MileSprout" (the capital S invites "Mile Sprout" as two words in search) and never "Miles Prout". (2) **The wordmark splits the colour at the right place**: "Mile" in ink, "sprout" in brand green (or mint on the gradient), so the eye reads *Mile·sprout*. (3) Say it **"mile-sprout"** with the stress on *sprout* in every video and voice-over, and make the founder's first TikTok literally say it slowly. (4) In fr/fr-CA the brand name is never translated or respelled, and the French store listing and onboarding never put "prout" at the end of a line where it can be read alone. (5) **Test it before buying anything** (§7): if French speakers hear the fart, we drop the name. |
| **The "spr" cluster** | Harder for some Hindi, Punjabi and Bengali speakers ("is-prout", "sa-prout"). | Acceptable: people will tap the icon, not say the name. Keep the name out of spoken calls to action ("Search Milesprout"); use the QR code and "search *mileage tracker HMRC*" in courier groups instead. |
| **"Sprout" is a busy word** | Sprout Social, Sprouts Farmers Market, many small apps. | A clearance question, not a brand one: see §7. In marketing, never use "Sprout" on its own as a nickname. |

## 2. Positioning, taglines, App Store metadata

### Positioning statement
For **couriers and self-employed drivers** in the UK, US, Canada and Australia who lose track of the miles they can claim, **Milesprout** is the **mileage tracker that turns every work mile into money you can claim**, one row per shift, in your own language. Unlike mileage apps that need an account and send your trips to their servers, **Milesprout tracks for free with no account, keeps your data on your phone, and tells you the moment a mile is missed.**

### Tagline options
| # | Tagline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every mile grows your claim.** | Honest (a claim, not cash), five simple words, translates literally into all 10 languages. One growth word, no pun overload. |
| 2 | Watch your miles grow. | Warmest. But it doesn't say *money*, so it needs the counter beside it. |
| 3 | Drive. Watch it grow. | Punchy, good for video end cards. "It" is vague in translation. |
| 4 | Miles in. Money back. | Strongest money hint, no plant at all. "Money back" overclaims a deduction, so it would need "to claim" in small print. Use only on UK social. |
| 5 | No account. Just drive. Watch it grow. | Extends the existing lead line. Too long for a store screenshot. |

**Recommended: "Every mile grows your claim."** It carries the brand story, can be proved (the counter is exactly that), works in non-native English, and translates without an idiom. Use "Watch your miles grow." as the short sign-off on video end cards, next to a real number.

In km markets the tagline stays "Every mile…" in English (a "mile" is a common idiom in AU/CA). In fr/es/pt-BR etc. translators should use the local word for the distance unit only if it reads naturally; the glossary entry is "keep the meaning: every distance you drive adds to the amount you can claim".

### App Store title and subtitle

Approach: **keep the title identical in all four countries** ("Milesprout: Mileage Tracker") so brand search, word of mouth and screenshots match everywhere, and the head term "mileage tracker" sits in the most heavily weighted field. **Carry over the playbook's regional subtitles unchanged**: they were already length-checked, keyword-planned (tax office + "log", so Apple combines "mileage" from the title with "log" into "mileage log") and checked so they don't copy a rival's wording. The rename doesn't need fresh keyword work, only a new name in front of it. No competitor or platform names anywhere.

| Store | Title (≤30) | Subtitle (≤30) |
|---|---|---|
| **GB** | `Milesprout: Mileage Tracker` (27) | `HMRC Log for Couriers & Trades` (30) |
| **US** | `Milesprout: Mileage Tracker` (27) | `Auto IRS Log for Gig Drivers` (28) |
| **CA** | `Milesprout: Mileage Tracker` (27) | `CRA Vehicle Logbook, Auto Km` (28) |
| **AU** | `Milesprout: Mileage Tracker` (27) | `ATO Car Log, Km & Tax Claims` (28) |

- Keyword fields: keep the playbook's lists (§1 of `launch-playbook.md`). Don't add "milesprout" (the app name is already indexed) or "sprout" (nobody searches it for mileage; it wastes characters).
- Optional test for AU only, via a custom product page rather than the main listing: title `Milesprout: Car Logbook & Km` (28), since "logbook" is the ATO's own word. Check the popularity of both in Apple Ads Advanced first.
- Promotional text still opens with "You'll know if a mile was missed." The name changes, not the promise.

## 3. The mark

### Concept
A **two-leaf seedling whose stem is a winding road**. The gold dot climbs the road and comes to rest at the top, where the leaves open: it's the car that has arrived and the bud that's about to grow. At the base, a faint soil line is where it started as a seed. It keeps every piece of equity from the leaf mark: the road with cream lane dashes, the dark road colour, the leaf gradient, the gold dot with its light ring, the soft shadow, the diagonal energy (the leaves point up and right).

| Leaves | vertical gradient `#77E8A0` (top) → `#24B359` (bottom). These are the current icon's leaf colours; add them to BRAND.md as **Leaf light / Leaf deep**. |

### How it reads at each size
| Size | Changes from the master |
|---|---|
| **1024pt** (store icon) | Everything: soil line, shadows, veins, ~9 dashes, ring. Treat this as the master; every other size is derived from it, not scaled down blindly. |

| **One-colour uses** (stamps, receipts, fax-quality PDF footers) | Single-ink silhouette: leaves and road solid, dashes and the dot's ring knocked out. |

### App icon composition
- Gradient background edge to edge, 135°, as now.
- Mark centred at the master geometry above. Optically it sits a touch high (the dot and the upper leaf carry weight at the top), which is intended: it reads as *rising*.
- **No signs, no text, no money symbols in the icon.** The signs live only in the animation.
- Contrast check: the leaves' lightest stop (`#77E8A0`) against the gradient's lightest (`#0E9F6E`) is the weakest pair; the leaf shadow fixes it. Don't remove the shadow at 1024.

### Splash screen
- Background **`#0B7A55`**, as now (`app.json` and `INTRO_BACKGROUND` in `launch-intro.tsx` must match).
- Image (`splash-icon.png`, 120pt wide, as now) shows **only frame 0 of the animation: the soil line and the gold dot sitting in it** (the seed), positioned exactly where they sit in the full mark's 100×100 box, with everything else transparent. Because the splash image and the animation share the same box, the hand-off is pixel-identical and the sprout grows out of the splash.
- No wordmark on the splash (Apple discourages text on launch screens; it would also flash before the localised UI).

### Wordmark
- "Milesprout" in Inter 800 (SF Pro Heavy in the app), slight negative tracking.
- On light: "Mile" ink `#14261F` + "sprout" brand green `#0B7A55`. On the gradient: "Mile" white + "sprout" mint `#D1FAE5`.
- Lock-up: mark to the left of the wordmark, mark height = 1.25 × cap height, gap = the dot's diameter.

## 4. Launch animation storyboard

**Timing frame (unchanged constants):** 80ms lead · `QUICK_DRIVE_MS` 1600ms · `QUICK_HOLD_MS` 1500ms · `FADE_MS` 300ms ≈ **3.5s** in total. The returning-user version is below; the first-launch version uses the same beats with `DRIVE_MS` 3200 / `HOLD_MS` 500.

Everything is keyed to the existing `drive` value (0→1) with `Easing.inOut(Easing.cubic)`, so the beats stay in step if the timing constants change. Approximate clock times are for the current easing. The cubic ease starts slowly (good: the seed "waking") and lands gently (good: the dot settles into the bud).

| # | Clock (ms) | `drive` | What happens | Notes for the developer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0–80 | — | **Seed in soil.** The splash's frame: gold dot half-sunk in the soil line, on `#0B7A55`. | Identical to `splash-icon.png`. |
| 1 | 80–450 | 0 → 0.05 | **The seed wakes.** The dot rises out of the soil (y −2 units) with a 1.0 → 1.08 → 1.0 scale pop. The existing lift/scale to 1.5× (`LOGO_LIFT`, `GROWN_SCALE`) starts here. The money counter begins to fade in (`opacity = drive × 3`). | Slow start from the ease; reads as a breath. |
| 2 | 450–1680 | 0.05 → 1.0 | **The stem/road grows upward with the dot at its tip.** The road is drawn by animating `strokeDashoffset` on the road path from its full length to 0; the dot is positioned at the path's point for `drive`. Lane dashes are revealed one dash-gap behind the dot (a second dash-offset mask on the dash path), so the car "lays" them as now. | Same technique as today's road; only the path changes. |
| 3 | ~600 / ~750 / ~850 | 0.14 / 0.29 / 0.44 | **Roadside signs pop in** beside the stem: **petrol** (left), **shops** (right), **café** (left). Each scales 0.3 → 1.0 as the dot approaches and shrinks away after it passes, as in `intro-scenery.tsx`. | Keep the existing glyphs and the `appear`/`leave` maths. Drop the `office` and `tree` entries: a tree beside a plant is noise, and the founder asked for the three signs. Keep signs at least 6 units clear of the leaves. |
| 4 | ~1005 | 0.70 | **First leaf unfurls** (lower, left) as the dot passes its base: scales from 0 at the leaf base, with a −25° → 0° rotation about the base, over 220ms, with a small overshoot (1.04). This is the first **milestone**: the counter passes 70% of the total. | Pin the transform origin to the leaf base (47, 54). |
| 5 | 450–1680 | 0.05 → 1.0 | **The money counter ticks up** in the user's currency, in step with `drive` ("£ found this tax year", existing copy and 1% steps). Miles/km beneath. | Unchanged logic. |
| 6 | ~1250 → 1680 | 0.92 → 1.0 | **Second leaf unfurls** (upper, right), same motion, slightly larger overshoot (1.06), timed so it's fully open as the dot arrives. **The dot settles on the top of the road** between the leaves, and its white ring draws in (r 5.6 → 7.6) over 150ms. Counter lands on the exact total. | This is the second milestone: the full total. |
| 7 | 1680–3180 | 1.0 | **Hold on the icon.** The frame now matches the app icon exactly (minus the background, which is the splash green). One gold glint: the dot scales 1.0 → 1.12 → 1.0 over 240ms at the start of the hold, then nothing moves. The counter and "+£X since you last looked" are readable. | `SEASON_HOLD_MS` (1900) still applies in seasonal weeks. |
| 8 | 3180–3480 | — | **Fade** to the app (`FADE_MS`). | Unchanged. |

**First launch (before set-up):** same beats over 3200ms with the demo month (400 mi / 650 km) as the counter. With the extra time, let the leaves' unfurl last 320ms each.

**Seasonal variants:** `SeasonalLeaf` becomes `SeasonalSprout`. Seasonal touches go on the leaves and signs (snow on the leaves, lights on the café), never on the dot. The festive season must **not** turn the dot or leaves into a Brussels sprout.

### Reduced-motion version (`useReducedMotion()` true)
- No climbing, no growing, no signs (the scenery is already hidden under reduced motion).
- At 0ms, cross-fade (opacity only, 200ms) from the splash's seed to the **final frame**: full sprout, both leaves open, dot at the top with its ring.
- The counter shows the final total immediately (no ticking), with "+£X since you last looked" if relevant.
- Hold 1500ms, then the 300ms fade, about **2.0s** in total. No scale pop, no glint, no overshoot.

## 5. Voice

Plain, warm, specific. Short sentences, numbers over adjectives. **At most one plant word per screen** ("grow", "sprout", "plant", "seed"), and on many screens none. The plant is in the picture; the words don't need to say it again. Never cute-ify money ("your money garden", "sprouty"). Write so it survives translation: no idioms, no wordplay that only works in English. Translators may drop the plant word if it reads oddly.

| # | Where | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Notification: shift ended | "Shift saved: 47 miles, worth £25.85 to claim. One row, done." |
| 2 | Notification: tracking stopped | "Tracking stopped at 14:12. Tap to add the miles you missed." |
| 3 | Notification: weekly summary (Monday) | "Last week your claim grew by £61.40. 112 work miles, 5 shifts." |

### 6.2 Message to current TestFlight testers
> **We're changing our name to Milesprout.** Another app already uses "MileMint", so the next build has a new name and icon (a little green sprout with the same yellow car). Nothing else changes: your drives stay on your phone, and you don't need to do anything. Thank you for testing. Reply if the new icon is hard to spot on your home screen.

Send it **only after the domains and handles in §6.4 are secured**, because 50 testers in WhatsApp groups is a public announcement.

### 6.3 Launch-day hook: launch fresh (recommended)
**Recommendation: launch fresh as Milesprout. Don't run "from MileMint to Milesprout".**
- We haven't launched publicly. 1.0 hasn't gone to the store yet, so outside the Founding 50 nobody knows the old name. A rename story tells the public about a name they never knew.
- "MileMint" belongs to another company's live app. Every "formerly MileMint" post sends searches to *their* listing and invites a confused-customer or trademark complaint.
- A launch has one story to tell, and ours is "Uber only sees Uber" and "You'll know if a mile was missed". A rename story uses up that one story on us instead of the driver.
- Handle the old name quietly: the TestFlight message above, one FAQ line on the site ("Were we MileMint in testing? Yes. Same app, new name."), and the `milemint://` alias.
- The new animation *is* the launch-day hook: a 6-second vertical video of the seed climbing to a real courier's real total ("Every mile grows your claim."), posted in courier groups with the missed-miles proof.

## 7. Risks and kill criteria

Clearance is necessary but not sufficient. **Drop Milesprout and go to the next shortlisted name (Mileleaf, which keeps the existing leaf mark unchanged) if any of these happen**, even after a clean clearance report:

| # | Kill criterion | How we measure it | Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The French "prout" problem is real** | 5-second test with at least 8 French speakers (France and Québec) plus a Québécois translator review: say the name once, ask them to repeat it and what it makes them think of. | **2 or more** of 8 hear or mention *prout* / a fart, or laugh. A fart joke in our second-biggest language is a permanent tax on every Canadian post. |
| 2 | **People can't type it after hearing it** | Same test, all groups (UK couriers, Hindi/Punjabi/Bengali, Polish/Romanian, French): say it once, ask them to type it into App Store search. | Fewer than **6 in 10** type something that finds the app (allowing "milesprout"/"mile sprout"). |
| 3 | **It reads as small, slow or a joke** | Ask "what does this app do?" and "would you trust it with your tax?" after showing the icon + name. | **3 or more** of 10 couriers say childish, gardening, food, kids' app or Brussels sprouts *before* money or driving. |
| 4 | **A SPROUT-formative mark owner objects** | Clearance report; opposition during the UKIPO/USPTO publication period; any letter. | Any credible objection in classes 9, 36 or 42 from an established SPROUT-brand (software, social media, finance or payroll) that would mean a dispute. We can't afford one, and the next name is ready. |
| 5 | **The App Store name or a near-twin is taken** | Phone search on the App Store in GB, US, CA and AU (the desk screen was US web search only). | Any live app called Milesprout, Mile Sprout or Milesprouts in a finance/business/navigation category. |
| 6 | **The .com can't be had** | Day-0 registration fails or the domain is held. | .com not obtainable for under about £500. (.app alone is acceptable only if the .com isn't an active business.) |
| 7 | **The mark fails at small sizes** | Show the 29pt and tinted icons on a real home screen next to 20 common apps; ask testers to find it. | Fewer than 8 of 10 find it within 3 seconds, or it's described as a "flower"/"lollipop" rather than a plant or road. Fix the mark first; drop the name only if the sprout idea itself is what fails. |

**Risks we accept (watch, don't kill):**
- The "Mile-" prefix is crowded and weak as a trade mark: the distinctiveness rests on "sprout". Accept it, and never use "Mile" alone as a short form.
- Brussels-sprout jokes in UK Decembers: manage with §1's rule (one knowing joke a year, never in the product).
- The "spr" cluster for some South Asian speakers: manage with QR codes and category-search calls to action rather than the spoken name.
- Growth language can overclaim: always "to claim", "estimated", "based on HMRC rates" (`14-launch-campaign.md` §4).

*This brief is marketing judgement, not legal advice. Trade-mark and domain decisions follow the paid clearance search described in `rename-shortlist.md`.*
