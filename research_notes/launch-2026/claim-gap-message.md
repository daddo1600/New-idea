# Launch message: "The platform only counts part of your driving"

*Added 2 Oct 2026. It comes from the Pro earnings screenshot scan. The scan puts the platform's own on-job distance next to the work miles MileSprout tracked over the same period.*

## The idea

> **Uber counted 140 miles. MileSprout found 212 you can claim.**

Uber, Deliveroo, DoorDash and the rest only see part of a driver's working day. Their summaries mostly count distance **on a job**. They leave out:

- driving to the first pickup or into a busy zone
- driving between jobs
- driving while switching between apps (multi-apping)

A driver who claims only from the platform figure is likely to under-claim. MileSprout logs every drive by itself, so it captures those miles. The scan shows the gap using the driver's **own** numbers.

This is the strongest single message we have. It's concrete, it's about money, and drivers can check it themselves in seconds.

## Rules before using it anywhere (must follow)

1. **The 140/212 figures are an illustration, not a result.** Until we have real numbers from testers who agree to share them (playbook W1: "collect real missed-miles numbers with permission"), always mark them as an example: "e.g.", "for example", or an on-screen "Example" label. Never write "drivers find 50% more miles" or anything similar without real data behind it. UK ads must be backed by evidence (ASA/CAP Code), and the US FTC and AU ACCC take the same line.
2. **Say "miles you may be able to claim", not "you can claim".** What counts as work driving differs by country and by situation:
   - **UK (HMRC, self-employed):** driving between jobs is business travel. Home to the first job depends on whether the driver has a fixed workplace. **[CHECK wording with an accountant]**
   - **US (IRS):** driving between work locations, and driving while working (going to pickups), is generally deductible. Commuting is not. Uber's annual US tax summary already shows "online miles", which include pickup driving. So in the US, compare against the **weekly trip distance**, not the tax summary. The gap there is mostly driving before going online and multi-apping. **[CHECK]**
   - **AU (ATO) and CA (CRA):** the rules on travel from home to the first pickup are stricter. **[CHECK before any AU or CA post]**
3. **Disclose that you're the founder** in every forum post or comment. Follow each group's promotion rules. Reddit and most Facebook groups ban plain promotion: give help first and only link where the rules allow it.
4. **We never post on anyone's behalf.** The founder posts personally.
5. **Before Pro launches, the scan comparison isn't in the free app.** Say so if asked ("part of Pro"). The free app still tracks all the miles.
