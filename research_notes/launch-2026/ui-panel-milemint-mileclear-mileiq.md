### "How did you hear about us?" MC's card vs IQ's survey (simulated)
- **MileClear:** a one-tap card with chips, dismissable, sitting on the home. 17 of 24 said they'd tap it; 4 found it in the way because it sits above Start Trip.
- **MileIQ:** a full-screen, 5-question survey during setup, with Continue disabled until you answer. 18 resented it, and 7 picked a random answer to escape.
- Panel consensus: ask once, after value, in one tap, and never block anything.

**Should MileMint add one? Yes, small and late:**
- **When:** after the first sorted work drive, or on day 3, whichever comes first. Show it once.
- **Where:** a dismissable card *below* the deductions card, never above the shift control.
- **Wording:** "Quick question: how did you find MileMint? Your answer stays on this iPhone unless you choose to send it."
- **Chips:** App Store · A friend or another driver · WhatsApp or driver group · Facebook · TikTok · Google · Other. Add a "Send to MileMint" button that opens a pre-filled email, or a single anonymous request with no ID.
- **Privacy:** store the answer locally only. Sending is opt-in, so the App Store label stays "Data not collected". If it's sent automatically, the label must change, which would break our main promise.

### Where MileMint honestly loses (simulated)
- **Looks at night:** MileClear's dark amber UI looks "premium" (14); our white list screens look plainer.
- **Obvious controls:** MileClear's Start Trip / Start Shift needs no explanation.
- **Free value:** MileClear tracks without a cap, against our 40 drives. Bike riders and US workers left on that basis.
- **Least confident users:** MileIQ's hand-holding won Brian and Tom.
- **Accountants:** we have no accountant portal; Helen sends portal-minded clients to MileClear.

*Simulated personas reasoning from static screenshots: directional input for design decisions, not evidence. Validate the top 3 recommendations with 5–8 real drivers before building.*
