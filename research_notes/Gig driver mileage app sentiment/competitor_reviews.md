# Competitor reviews: mileage-tracking apps used by gig drivers (sentiment, pricing, positioning)

Research date: 2026-10-01.

**Method caveat (read first):** This sandbox's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches of apps.apple.com, Google Play, trustpilot.com, justuseapp.com, mwm.ai, gridwise.io, therideshareguy.com and keptmiles.app, and the iTunes Search API was blocked too. ## Q1. Competitor profiles: price, free tier, platforms, praise, complaints, ratings, gig positioning

### Takeaway
The market splits three ways. (a) Gig-native "driver assistant" apps (Gridwise, Solo, Para, Stride) sync earnings and target US rideshare and delivery drivers. (b) General mileage loggers (MileIQ, Everlance, Driversnote, TripLog, Hurdlr) cap free tiers at 15–40 auto-detected drives a month or keep automatic tracking behind a paywall. (c) Accounting suites (QuickBooks Solopreneur, FreeAgent, Xero) treat mileage as a secondary feature. Across all groups, users complain most about price rises (especially MileIQ) and about missed or partial trips plus battery drain (Stride, Everlance, Gridwise, Driversnote).

### Cited Findings

**MileIQ** (Microsoft-owned at one point; iOS and Android)
- Free tier: 40 drives/month. Unlimited plan: $11.66/mo billed annually or $13.99/mo billed monthly. — [Timeero MileIQ Review 2026](https://timeero.com/reviews/mileiq-review); [magica-app.com MileIQ alternatives 2026](https://magica-app.com/best-mileiq-alternatives-2026/)
- Reported price history: "since 2025 the monthly rate for individual users rose from $5.99 to $13.99, while the annual plan increased from $59.99 to $139.92". Another source puts it at $72/yr to $168/yr (+133%) between 2024 and 2026. The two figures differ, so take the size of the rise as approximate. — [TripLog blog: MileIQ alternatives 2026](https://www.triplog.net/blog/mileiq-alternatives); [MileageWise MileIQ alternative](https://www.mileagewise.com/mileiq-alternative/)
- UK: Unlimited costs £7.91/mo billed annually (~£95/yr) or £9.49/mo billed monthly. — [MileIQ UK pricing](https://mileiq.com/pricing-uk)
- Reported user quotes about price (App Store, Google Play and Trustpilot, as relayed by search): "The monthly fees have increased 3x in 3 years and now I'm looking for another app."; "The price just keeps going up there are better cheaper options"; "And on top of that the price point is a little insane to me. $20 - $30 sure– but $90+?"; "I have been a happy MileIQ user for years, but doubling the annual subscription from £45 to £90 is completely unjustifiable."; "The app has not had any new features or improvements to justify the price hike except for greed. I could understand a 2-5% hike, but a 70% hike is just corp greed." — [Trustpilot MileIQ](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/mileiq.com); [Trustpilot CA p2](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/mileiq.com?page=2); [MileageWise MileIQ review](https://www.mileagewise.com/mileiq-alternative/mileiq-review/)
- Reliability complaints: misses short trips (drives under half a mile are ignored), struggles offline, can overestimate mileage, some drives appear only later, and tracking is unreliable after a phone change. — [Timeero MileIQ Review 2026](https://timeero.com/reviews/mileiq-review); [magica-app.com](https://magica-app.com/best-mileiq-alternatives-2026/)
- Billing and support: complaints of double billing, unauthorised charges and poor support. PissedConsumer rating is 1.6 stars, with 33% recommending. — [PissedConsumer MileIQ](https://mileiq.pissedconsumer.com/review.html)
- Praise: still seen as the "set it and forget it" auto-tracker. — [Timeero iPhone mileage trackers 2026](https://timeero.com/post/iphone-mileage-tracker)
- Gig positioning: generic small business and self-employed. It has no earnings import and no shift features (no source says otherwise).

**Everlance** (iOS and Android; App Store id985378916)
- Free: 30 auto-detected trips/month plus unlimited manual start/stop. Premium is $8/mo billed annually; Premium Plus is $12/mo billed annually and adds bank/card sync. Gridwise's comparison lists Everlance at "$8.99/month", which conflicts with the $8 figure. — [Capterra Everlance pricing](https://www.capterra.com/p/174335/Everlance/pricing/); [Gridwise vs Everlance vs Stride](https://gridwise.io/blog/gridwise-vs-everlance-vs-stride)
- Praise: automates tracking, user-friendly, prompt support, IRS-compliant logs. — [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/174335/Everlance/reviews/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/everlance/reviews)
- Complaints (paraphrased by search): it captures only a segment of a trip or misses it entirely; it "arbitrarily" records trips even on Premium; phone support is unhelpful; prices rose; it drains the battery; it shuts off after being idle. — [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/174335/Everlance/reviews/); [Timeero Everlance review](https://timeero.com/reviews/everlance-review); [justuseapp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/985378916/everlance-mileage-expense/reviews)

**Stride** (Stride Health; iOS id1041591359 and Android)
- Price: completely free, with unlimited mileage and expense tracking. It is funded by its health-insurance marketplace and "everyday deals". — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stride-mileage-tax-tracker/id1041591359); [Gridwise comparison](https://gridwise.io/blog/gridwise-vs-everlance-vs-stride)
- Marketing claims: "save an average of $710/year on taxes"; IRS-ready logs; "automated GPS mileage tracking and reminders". It targets rideshare and delivery drivers. — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stride-mileage-tax-tracker/id1041591359)
- Google Play rating: about 3.1, described as low against peers. — [chrome-stats Stride reviews](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.stridehealth.drive/reviews); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.stridehealth.drive)
- Complaints: described as "a really bad battery hog"; poor with battery-saver mode; missed chunks of miles; stops running in the background mid-day; merges stops into one round trip; data lost after updates or reinstalls; Android shows "no internet connection" errors and trips disappear. — [EntreCourier Stride review](https://entrecourier.com/delivery/delivery-strategies/delivery-tools/stride-tax-app-review-2021/); [MileageWise Stride review](https://www.mileagewise.com/mileiq-alternative/stride-review/); [justuseapp Stride](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1041591359/stride-mileage-tax-tracker/reviews)

**Gridwise** (iPhone App Store id1215991382 and Android)

- Paywall change: from 18 April 2024, automatic mileage tracking and earnings syncing became Plus-only. Basic users' earnings stopped syncing. ### Inferences
- MileIQ's 40-drive free cap is the reference point that MileMint's "40 drives/month free" copies. MileIQ's price rise, from about $5.99 to $13.99/mo (or about £95/yr in the UK), is the most cited grievance in the category. A Pro tier at about £/$5.99 lands near TripLog Premium and MileIQ's old price, which is a clear "what MileIQ used to cost" message.
- Missed trips, partial trips and battery drain are complaints shared by every auto-tracker (MileIQ, Everlance, Stride, Gridwise, Driversnote). No app owns the "trust" position yet. MileMint's missed-miles check against the delivery app's count speaks directly to this pain, and I found no competitor offering it.
- Gridwise's April 2024 paywall shows that taking away free features (earnings sync, auto-mileage) causes lasting resentment.

## Q2. Which competitor is closest to MileMint, and where is MileMint weak?

### Takeaway
On features, Gridwise is the closest: gig-native, shift-aware and deadhead-capturing, with a 4.9 App Store rating. On the free-cap and price model, MileIQ is the closest. Outside the US, Driversnote overlaps most with MileMint's multi-country, multi-rate pitch (IRS, HMRC, CRA and ATO). MileMint is weaker on earnings import, Android, brand trust and review volume.

### Cited Findings
- Gridwise: gig-native, multi-platform earnings sync, deadhead capture; iPhone 4.9 from ~28K ratings — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gridwise-gig-driver-assistant/id1215991382); [Gridwise comparison](https://gridwise.io/blog/gridwise-vs-everlance-vs-stride)
- Driversnote: multi-country rates (IRS/HMRC/CRA/ATO), iOS, Android and web, iBeacon car detection — [Front Desk Review](https://frontdeskreview.com/software/mileage-tracking/driversnote/)
- MileIQ: 40 drives/month free cap — [Timeero](https://timeero.com/reviews/mileiq-review)
- Stride: free and unlimited, gig-targeted, but poorly reviewed on Android (~3.1) — [chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.stridehealth.drive/reviews)

### Inferences
- MileMint's likely weaknesses against these competitors: (1) iPhone-only, while Gridwise, Stride, Solo, Everlance, Driversnote and TripLog all run on Android, and many couriers use Android. (2) No earnings import or offer analysis (Gridwise, Solo and Para have it). (3) Stride is free and unlimited, and TripLog has free unlimited auto-tracking (paying only for reports), so a 40-drive cap is stingier for a high-volume courier, who can exceed 40 drives a month within days. (4) No hardware or Bluetooth beacon option like Driversnote's. (5) A new app has no review base, while incumbents hold thousands of ratings.
- MileMint's possible strengths: the missed-miles reconciliation (no competitor found), 10 languages (most competitors are English-first), four tax regimes in one gig app (Gridwise, Solo and Para are US-only), and a lower Pro price than MileIQ, Gridwise or Driversnote.
- A note on the cap: if a "drive" means a single detected trip, couriers doing multi-drop shifts will hit 40 very quickly. Counting shift mode as one drive, or capping by shifts, could avoid the paywall resentment seen with MileIQ and Gridwise.
