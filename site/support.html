Common questions

MileMint isn't detecting my drives.

Open Settings → MileMint → Location and choose Always, and make sure Background App Refresh is on. Low Power Mode can delay detection. You can always add a missed trip by hand with the + button. If tracking stops, MileMint tells you and offers to add the missed trip.

Will it drain my battery?

MileMint uses low-power geofencing while you're parked and switches GPS on only while you're driving. You can see its share of battery use in Settings → Battery.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, encrypted. We never receive your trips or location. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

If you're signed in to iCloud, MileMint backs up your trips to your own iCloud, encrypted, by itself (check Settings → Backup in the app). Install MileMint on the new iPhone, signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Keychain on, and the first screen offers to restore your trips. Restoring your whole iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup works too.

I lost my phone. Are my trips gone?

Not if MileMint's iCloud backup was on: the backup is in your iCloud and its key is in your iCloud Keychain. Install MileMint on your new phone and choose Restore my trips.

What does "one row per shift" mean?

When you start a shift, every drive until you end it is grouped into one row, so a day of deliveries doesn't become forty separate entries. The drive home after your shift is split off, and you choose whether it counts as business.

Which rates does MileMint use?
  • UK: HMRC approved mileage rates, 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles in the tax year (from 6 April 2026), then 25p.
  • US: IRS standard mileage rate, 72.5¢ a mile for January to June 2026 and 76¢ from July 2026.
  • Canada: a CRA logbook with your business-use percentage, plus CRA's per-kilometre allowance rate for reference.
  • Australia: the ATO cents per kilometre method, 91c per km for 2026–27, up to 5,000 km per car.

How do I get my records out?

MileMint makes reports for your tax office and exports to a spreadsheet, Xero, QuickBooks and (in the UK) FreeAgent. Free to track. Pro for reports: what's included in each plan will be shown in the app and on the App Store at launch.

Is this tax advice?

No. MileMint estimates your claim using the official rates from HMRC, the IRS, the CRA or the ATO. Check with an accountant or tax agent for your situation.
