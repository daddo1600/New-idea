Questions

Is it free?

Free to track. Pro for reports. Automatic tracking and your mileage log are free; MileMint Pro adds the reports you need at tax time. Prices will be shown on the App Store at launch, and the Founding 1,000 keep their launch price of Pro for as long as they subscribe.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Your trips are stored encrypted on your iPhone, and if you use iCloud, an encrypted backup goes to your own iCloud. We never receive your trips or your location.

Which countries does it work in?

The United Kingdom (HMRC), the United States (IRS), Canada (CRA) and Australia (ATO). MileMint uses each tax office's rates, tax year and record-keeping rules.

Which languages?

The app comes in 10 languages: English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Română, Polski, हिन्दी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, বাংলা and 简体中文.

Does it drain my battery?

MileMint uses iOS location services the efficient way: low-power geofencing while you're parked, and GPS only while you're driving. Any app that detects drives in the background uses some battery, and you can always see MileMint's share in Settings → Battery. If you prefer, switch to manual logging at any time.

Is this tax advice?

No. MileMint estimates your claim using the official rates. Check with an accountant or tax agent for your situation.
