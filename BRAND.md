# MileMint brand: use this for everything customers or partners see

App, App Store images, website, emails, slides, flyers, social posts.

## Colours
| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Brand gradient (hero backgrounds, statement slides), 135° | `#0E9F6E` → `#053D2E` |
| Brand green (buttons, highlights, links on light) | `#0B7A55` |
| Deep green (text on light green, dark accents) | `#064E3B` |
| Gold (eyebrows, the one key word in a headline, badges, underlines) | `#FACC15` (always dark text on gold) |
| Mint (body text on the green gradient) | `#D1FAE5` |
| Cream (light backgrounds, cards on green) | `#FBF7EE` |
| Light green tint (highlighted cells/rows) | `#DDF3E8` |
| Ink (headings on light) | `#14261F`; body `#33443D`; secondary `#5B6B64` |

Never pure `#FFFFFF`/`#000000` backgrounds except white text on the gradient.

## Type
- iPhone app: the system font (SF Pro). Everywhere else: **Inter** (Google Fonts), the closest match.
- Headlines 800 weight, slight negative tracking; eyebrows UPPERCASE, 800, letter-spaced, gold.
- One gold word or underline per headline at most ("was **missed**.", "**forgot.**").

## Logo
- The sprout mark: a seedling whose stem is the road (cream lane dashes), with the gold dot (the car) at the top where the leaves open. Drawn from `milemint/src/brand/sprout.ts`; exports `milemint/assets/brand/mark.svg` (transparent) and `mark-small.svg` (under 60pt: no veins, sheen, shadow or soil), app icon `icon.png`, splash `splash-seed.png` (the seed in its soil, the launch animation's first frame).
- Leaf colours: Leaf light `#77E8A0` → Leaf deep `#24B359` (vertical gradient), underside `#085E42`, veins Deep green.
- Wordmark "MileMint": white on green; on light, "Mile" ink + "Mint" brand green.

## Voice
- Plain, warm, specific; short sentences; numbers over adjectives.
- Lead lines: "You'll know if a mile was missed." · "No account. Just drive." · "Set up in under a minute."
- Never name competitors on the App Store; no claims we can't prove (ratings, user counts, "#1").
- UK spelling for UK material.

## Layout feel
- Big green statement areas, cream content cards with soft shadow (radius 24–28px), generous space, one idea per screen/slide.
- Store screenshot style: cream background, ink headline with a gold underline under the key word, phone with an enlarged card floating over it.
